🆕 NEW (1)
🆕 The last action before a credit purchase is an agent conversation, not a billing-page visit — top-ups are ~2x more often reactive/in-chat than deliberate
Mixpanel Flows into credit_topup (prod, last 6mo, unique, N~128 toppers; event history since ~May 2026) shows the single most common IMMEDIATE predecessor of a top-up is agent_response_received — 55 users (44%) top up mid-conversation — vs the deliberate billing path page_visited_settings_billing -> button_billing_agregar_creditos (~24-25, ~19%). Two steps back, 52 users went agent_message_sent -> agent_response_received -> topup with NO billing page in the last two actions. So credit top-up is predominantly a REACTIVE hit-the-credit-wall-in-chat event (~2x the deliberate billing path). This stresses the Jul-23 read that page_visited_settings_billing IS the pay-intent signal: it is a clean intent signal but captures only ~1 in 5 actual top-up MOMENTS; a billing-page-anchored view misses the ~2 in 5 top-ups that fire straight out of a conversation. Device lens: top-ups are 85% desktop (Windows 77 + Mac 27 of 128; mobile just 23/18%) even though mobile is ~half of signups and converses at 60% — the payers are the desktop-heavy engaged cohort. Marks not drives; distinct_id not company-deduped; credit_topup is pre-checkout intent-to-pay (credits only), reconciled revenue/MRR still Stripe-deferred.
Metric: share of credit_topup whose last action is an agent conversation (vs billing page) = 44
Sources: mixpanel
Confidence: medium
Caveat: Flows into credit_topup, prod, 6mo, unique, stepsBefore up to 2; N~128 unique toppers (~126 with a traceable predecessor). Immediate predecessor: agent_response_received 55 (44%), add-credits button 34 (27%, the mechanical final click — 24 of those preceded by the billing page), app_session_started 17 (13%), billing page 3, node_used 3, tester 3, others 2 each. Two-step: message->response->topup 52; billing-page->add-credits->topup 24. Flows adjacency = immediately-preceding events (tighter than a time-bounded 48h window). agent_response_received is system-side but is the tail of a user-initiated message->response pair, so the 52-chain is real conversation, not automation. Sub-splits small-N; the 52-vs-24 and 85%-desktop reads are robust. Context (not a finding): Jul toppers fell 58->22 but agent monthly actives fell 1596->663 (both ~-60%), so topup-per-active held ~3.5% — Jul drop is downstream of the known signup/activity decline, not monetization-specific.
