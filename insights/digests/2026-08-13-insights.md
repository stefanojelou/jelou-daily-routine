### 🆕 Re-engagement lives on the CANVAS, not the chat — for chat-activated users, weekly return-via-BUILD overtakes return-via-chat by ~week 3-4 and decays ~1.6x slower

Today assigned angle -- resurrection: who comes back and what marks the comeback -- has a clean, novel answer in a cross-surface 2x2 retention matrix (prod, weekly curves, births Jun 1-Aug 12 2026, cohort on each surface event, count the RETURN event on either surface). For the wide chat cohort (agent_message_sent, avg ~75 births/day), same-surface return-to-chat decays fast -- wk1 25%, wk3 19%, wk4 15%, wk6 13%, wk9 9% -- but return-to-BUILD (node_used) on the SAME cohort starts a touch lower and decays much slower: wk1 22%, wk3 19%, wk4 17%, wk6 17%, wk9 13%. So the two curves CROSS at ~wk3-4 and build leads every week after, with the gap widening (+2pt wk4 -> +4pt wk6/wk9): a chat-activated user who has been away several weeks is MORE likely to come back on the builder than in the chat. Decay-rate makes it sharper -- chat return keeps only 36% of its wk1 level by wk9 (25->9) while build return keeps 59% (22->13), i.e. chat re-engagement fades ~1.6x faster than build re-engagement. The build surface is the gravitational center of comebacks on BOTH cohorts: builders (node_used, ~59 births/day) return to build at 44/40/33/24% (wk1/3/6/9) -- roughly 2x the chat cohorts return-to-chat and the most durable curve measured -- and even builders return-to-CHAT (31/25/20/15%) beats chatters own return-to-chat (25/19/13/9%), so building marks the stickier population on every surface. Campaign_sent is decisively NOT the comeback surface (chat cohort return-via-campaign is a flat 3-5% all weeks), ruling out outbound-campaign activity as the resurrection trigger. Net reframe of resurrection: comebacks concentrate on the canvas -- the intervention that recovers a dormant user is a reason to BUILD, not another chat. Marks not drives.
