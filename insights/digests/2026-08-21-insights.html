Pivoting to the one pay signal Mixpanel CAN see, the genuinely-new read is that credit_topup (the pre-settlement payment/intent-to-pay event) COLLAPSED alongside the Jul-3 acquisition break and never recovered: weekly UNIQUE buyers fell from a ~15.5/wk May-Jun baseline (16,28,16,8,8,17) to ~3/wk across the next 7 weeks (2,6,2,2,3,4,2) = ~-80% (total events -86%, 23->3/wk), with the week of Jul 6-12 essentially flatlining to ~0 topups. Crucially this is the DEEPEST contraction anywhere in the funnel: over the same 12 weeks weekly UNIQUE users fell app_session_started -20% (2,132->1,712), agent_message_sent -65% (468->162), page_visited_settings_billing (the billing INTENT signal) only -41% (117->69), but credit_topup -80%. So the drop-off concentrates at the FINAL pay step, not at intent: the billing-intent signal held up ~2x better than the actual top-up, and even the aggregate weekly topup-per-billing-viewer ratio fell ~13% -> ~5% (roughly a third as many billing visitors convert to a top-up as before). This is NOT a dead tag (credit_topup still fires 2-6/wk and billing-page views continue firing 100-121/day through mid-July), so it is a REAL contraction of the payment motion, most likely the monetization echo of the ~80% new-user acquisition collapse (Aug-6) working through the active base -- and it also QUALIFIES the Aug-6 'installed base 100% healthy' read: total app-sessions held near baseline, but weekly UNIQUE actives and unique payers both fell 60-80%. Net for monetization: since early July there are ~3 credit buyers/week in all of production -- the Mixpanel-visible pay surface is now too thin to segment by media or plan, so the entire monetization theme is effectively Stripe-blocked until reconciled revenue is wired.
