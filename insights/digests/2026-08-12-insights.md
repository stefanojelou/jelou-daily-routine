## 🆕 New (2)

### 🆕 Credit-toppers retain ~4x better than subscribers on near-term agent activity -- and the two are ~94% disjoint populations, not a funnel

Attempting the Jul-14-deferred angle now that subscription_changed has ~7 weeks of history: on the clean co-existing window (Jun 22-Aug 12, prod), cohorting on each pay event and measuring return via agent_message_sent, credit-toppers (N=35) out-retain subscribers (N=25) at every early week -- same-week 60% vs 26%, wk1 24% vs 6%, wk2 23% vs 6%, wk3 14% vs 8%, wk4 15% vs 9%. So on the only monetization-retention proxy Mixpanel can measure, the credit-topup path marks materially stickier near-term product usage. BUT the proxy is invalid for SUBSCRIBERS: only ~1 in 4 subscribers even sends an agent message in the week they subscribe, consistent with subscribers being a different (likely sales-sold/managed) motion whose real retention is MRR-continuation (Stripe, deferred) -- so this almost certainly UNDER-measures subscriber retention. Honest read: measured by return-to-agent-usage, toppers look far stickier; true subscription retention needs Stripe. Unblocked on TIME, still fragile on N.

- **Metric:** **subscriber wk1 agent-activity retention, coexist window (vs credit-topper 24) = 6**
- **Sources:** mixpanel
- **Confidence:** low-medium
- **Caveat:** Weekly retention curves, prod, cohort on the pay event, return=agent_message_sent, Jun 22-Aug 12 2026 (co-existing window; subscription_changed launched ~Jun 24). Dedup populations TINY: credit_topup 35 / subscription_changed 25 (ratio ~1.4:1, was 1.7:1 on Jul-14). wk0 (60 vs 26) is the most robust cell; wk1/wk2 rest on ~1-2 returners of ~20 mature subscriber births so are directional. KEY: agent_message_sent under-measures subscribers (only 26% message in subscribe-week) -> topper>subscriber gap is partly a proxy artifact. credit_topup = pre-checkout intent (credits only), not reconciled revenue. distinct_id, not company-deduped. Revisit when N grows / Stripe wired. Marks not drives.

### 🆕 Credit-toppers and subscribers are ~94% DISJOINT populations, not two stages of one funnel

Answering the backlog question on the confound-free co-existing window (Jun 22-Aug 12, prod, unique, 45d conversion): of 35 credit-toppers only 2 (6%) ever fire subscription_changed, and of 25 subscribers only 2 (8%) ever fire credit_topup. ~94% of each monetization group never touches the other -- credit top-ups and subscriptions are two largely SEPARATE motions (different buyers), not sequential stages. This refines the prior lifetime-confounded estimate (3% topup->sub, 14% sub->topup, both distorted by subscription_changed only starting ~Jun 24): on the fair window the two directions are near-symmetric and tiny. Time twist (N=2 each, anecdotal): topup->sub crossovers took ~11.9 days; sub->topup crossovers happened same-session (~28 min).

- **Metric:** **credit-topper -> subscription_changed 45d conversion, coexist window (sub->topup = 8%) = 6**
- **Sources:** mixpanel
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Caveat:** Funnels, prod, unique, 45d window, Jun 22-Aug 12 2026. topup->sub 35->2 (6%); sub->topup 25->2 (8%). Ordered funnels catch only A-before-B; total any-order overlap <= ~2-4 of ~56-58 distinct payers, so ~90%+ disjoint. Small N so exact %s directional; disjoint conclusion robust (single-digit crossovers both ways). Crossover times N=2 each (anecdotal). credit_topup pre-settlement intent; subscription_changed newborn since ~Jun 24; MRR is Stripe-only (deferred). distinct_id, not company-deduped.
