🆕 Deep builders are a NESTED subset of deep conversers, not a disjoint population — 68% of top-decile building companies are also top-decile conversers, but only 23% the reverse (build ⊂ converse)
Answering the backlog question directly -- do the ~10% power-user cores on node_used (BUILD) and agent_message_sent (CONVERSE) overlap at company grain, or are builders and conversers disjoint account sets? -- the answer is NEITHER disjoint NOR symmetric: deep building is a NESTED INNER CORE inside the broader conversation population. On per-company total-event distributions (prod, last 90d, company_id $user profile; node 1,033 companies / 18,188 attributed node-adds, agent 2,988 companies / ~29,549 msgs), 68.0% of top-decile building companies (70/103) are ALSO top-decile conversing companies, and 95% (98/103) send at least one agent message -- i.e. almost every deep builder is also a deep (or at least active) converser. The REVERSE is weak and asymmetric: only 23.4% of top-decile conversing companies (70/299) are also top-decile builders, and only 78% of them add any node at all -- most deep conversers never build deeply. The same nesting holds at the top 1% (elite): 60% of the 10 build-elite are agent-elite vs only 20% of the 30 agent-elite are build-elite. At the ANY-activity level the asymmetry repeats -- of 1,033 companies that build, 789 (76%) also converse; of 2,988 that converse, only 789 (26%) also build. And the volume mirrors it: the 789 dual-active companies hold 92.6% of ALL node-adds but only 68.1% of all agent messages -- building volume lives almost entirely inside dual-active accounts, while ~32% of conversation volume sits in a chat-ONLY tail (2,199 companies, 74% of all conversers, that never add a single node). Correlation confirms the shape: across the union (0-fill, N=3,232) Spearman rho is only 0.148 -- dragged down by the huge chat-only tail -- but AMONG the 789 companies that do both, log-log Pearson r = 0.545 (moderate: once a company builds at all, its build depth and chat depth rise together). Net reframe of the Aug-5 power-law: builders and conversers are not two separate power-user populations -- the build core is a strict, deeper subset (build ⊂ converse). Sizing the leak (today's twist): ~2,200 companies engage the assistant purely via chat and never touch the canvas builder -- the single biggest untapped 'converse-but-never-build' pool -- vs only 244 companies that build without conversing. Marks not drives: heavy builders by construction do more of everything, so the nesting reflects an engaged inner core, not that building causes conversing.
