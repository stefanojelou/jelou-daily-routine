🆕 Mobile conversation is a same-session dead-end -- mobile-first conversers retain wk1 at ~0.4% (3/787) vs desktop 34%, an ~80x gap, despite activating the chat at ~59%
Directly answering the open backlog question -- do mobile-signup messagers RETAIN, or converse once and vanish? -- the answer is VANISH, and the gap vs desktop is enormous. On weekly self-retention cohorted on a user's first agent_message_sent by the DEVICE of that first message (prod, June 2026 clean pre-collapse window, return=agent_message_sent on ANY device), mobile-first conversers (Android, N~=787 June births) send a 2nd message the same week 62% of the time but then essentially never come back: wk1 ~0.4% (~3 users), wk2-4 all <1%. Desktop-first conversers (Windows, N~=1,322) retain wk1 34%, wk2 28%, wk4 21%, wk8 11% -- a durable curve. So mobile conversation is NOT real activation: it is a one-session event with ~0% week-over-week return, ~80x below desktop. This reframes the ledger's known mobile-vs-desktop ACTIVATION gap (mobile converses ~60% vs desktop ~69%, Jul-6) -- the small activation gap hides a near-total RETENTION collapse: mobile users do reach the assistant and even multi-message in the first session (62% wk0), they just have nowhere to go afterwards (node_used is desktop-only, so they cannot build), and they do not migrate to desktop either (the return event is unfiltered by device, so any later message on any device WOULD count, and it is still ~0%). Net: mobile chat volume is almost entirely non-retaining, and the retention base is a desktop phenomenon. Marks not drives -- device is confounded with channel (media is ~77% mobile) and geography, so 'mobile' here also proxies the casual media/LatAm cohort; but the outcome is the same for the retention math.

🆕 Delayed activators barely exist on mobile -- mobile signups are same-day-or-never (59% day-0, ~0 tail -> ~60% cumulative) while desktop keeps a slow trickle (68% day-0 -> ~75% cumulative)
Addressing the assigned 'day-0 vs delayed activation' angle by device: agent activation is overwhelmingly SAME-DAY, but the small 'delayed activator' tail is almost entirely a DESKTOP behavior. On the signup->first-agent-message time distribution (prod, June, cohort on signup_completed by entry device, first-message measured on any device), mobile (Android) signups activate 59% in the signup WEEK and add essentially nothing afterward (~60% cumulative -- a pure same-day-or-never pattern), whereas desktop (Windows) signups activate 68% in week 0 AND keep converting a slow trickle across later weeks to ~75% cumulative. So delayed (day-1+) activation is ~10x more common on desktop (~7% of desktop signups vs ~0.7% of mobile signups). This means the literal assigned question -- does day-0 beat day-1..7 on retention? -- is nearly moot on mobile (there is no delayed cohort to nudge) and is a desktop-only opportunity, but even that desktop delayed tail is small (~7% of signups). Combined with insight 1 (mobile chat ~0% wk1 retention), the actionable read is that WHERE a user activates (device) dominates WHEN (day-0 vs delayed): the retention lever is getting users onto the desktop builder, not accelerating a day-1 chat. Confirms the ledger's known overall speed CDF (Jul-17: 60% activate by 1d, 65% by 30d) and adds the never-before-measured DEVICE split of that timing.
